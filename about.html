What is an Electric Vehicle?

An electric vehicle (EV) is a vehicle that uses an electric motor instead of a petrol or diesel engine.

What is EV Charging?

EV charging means supplying electricity to an electric vehicle's battery. It can be done at home or at public charging stations.

How Does EV Charging Work?

An electric vehicle is connected to a charging point using a charging cable. Electricity is supplied to the battery and stored for driving.
