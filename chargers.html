AC Charger

AC chargers are commonly used at homes. They supply AC electricity, which the vehicle converts into DC to charge its battery. They generally take several hours.

DC Charger

DC chargers are commonly found at public charging stations. They supply DC electricity directly to the battery and can charge much faster than AC chargers.

AC vs DC Chargers

  • AC charging is generally slower.
  • DC charging is generally faster.
  • AC chargers are common for home charging.
  • DC chargers are common at public stations.
